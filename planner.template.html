Drag a tent onto the plan, or click it then click the plan. Each tent is placed once and leaves the list. Double-click a placed tent to edit it. Select a placed tent to rotate (left / right arrows, Shift = fine, or the rotate buttons); delete it to return it to the list.
Electrical view. Add a Source (a popup sets its amps and outputs) and cabinets from the toolbar. To wire, turn on the Wire tool, click a source/cabinet, then click what it feeds. With Wire off, clicking just selects and highlights the supply chain. Cabinets are three-phase: single-phase 220V loads are balanced across L1/L2/L3, three-phase loads sit on all phases, and any phase over the rating is flagged red. With Wire on: click a cable to add a dot you can drag to bend it; double-click the dot to switch sharp/curve, right-click it to remove; double-click the cable (or right-click) to recolour or remove the whole wire. Cable lengths show at each wire's midpoint (Lengths toggle) and are totalled below. Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Shift+Z redoes. Select a node and press Delete to remove it.
Water view. Add a Brunn (water source) and Handfat (sink) from the toolbar, then turn on the Wire tool and click a brunn followed by a handfat or water object to run a blue pipe. With Wire on: click a pipe to add a blue dot you can drag to bend it; double-click the dot to switch sharp/curve, right-click it to make it a gray koppling or remove it. A koppling is a junction: click it and then a tent/handfat to branch a new pipe from that point (right-click removes it with its branches). Double-click the pipe (or right-click) to recolour or remove it. Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Shift+Z redoes. Select a fixture and press Delete.

Power source

Set the rating and which outputs this unit provides. The rating applies per phase (L1/L2/L3), so a 63A unit carries 63A on each of L1/L2/L3 (three 32A tents fit easily, one per phase).

Wire colour

Pick a colour for this wire. By default a wire takes its cabinet's rating colour (32A orange, 16A yellow, 63A/125A red) with a black outline.

Wire dot

A square dot is a sharp bend, a round dot pulls a smooth curve through it. On a water pipe a gray koppling is a junction that other pipes can branch from. Drag the dot on the map to move it; double-click it to switch sharp/curve without opening this window.
